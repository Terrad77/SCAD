# v0.10 — governed production issues

SelfCheck findings remain diagnostics. An explicit `issues sync` snapshots only a
current, signed production audit after independent recomputation. It does not run
as a feedback loop. `production-issues` is a separate, signed, narrowly scoped
operational journal, not a reasoning decision ledger. Finding identity includes
the complete production snapshot and diagnostic; historical findings are retained.

An operator chooses fix, research, reasoning, defer, or dismiss and supplies a
rationale. Every new disposition requires explicit human approval of its exact
snapshot and action. Authorization is persisted before an effect. Resume replays
only an authorized pending operation. Concurrent writers are unsupported, as in
production revisions. Corrupt journals and outdated production fail closed.

Fix uses the existing v0.8 narrative → visual → SelfCheck revision workflow,
including its independent stage/final checkpoints and crash recovery. The issue
stores its plan and completed revision ID. It does not implement another publisher.
The conservative initial scope starts at narrative; selective shot-only editing
and a richer interface are excluded.

Research/reasoning requests append idempotently to the existing `decisions` store,
with an immutable issue/snapshot reference and the human rationale. The diagnostic
is audit metadata, never policy input or evidence. This increment queues a human
request for an explicitly invoked existing reasoning cycle; it does not promise
execution of the requested research or override deterministic action selection.
Issue requests must not answer paused human-input checkpoints. Their governance
trigger must be consumed once when an explicit new cycle starts, including recovery.

Defer and dismiss are dispositions, not successful audit results. Fix and handoff
leave an issue awaiting verification. Explicit verify requires a different current
snapshot, absence of the same finding family, and PASS for its relevant independent
check. UNKNOWN cannot close an issue. Regeneration alone cannot close anything.

Scope excludes autonomous feedback, diagnostic-derived evidence, confidence
accumulation, a second reasoning engine or governance ledger, GUI and media.

Architectural limits: one writer, single-file atomic journals rather than a
multi-file transaction; content signatures detect accidental drift, not hostile
forgery. Finding families use kind and subject IDs and cannot prove unrestricted
semantic equivalence. Legacy or unverifiable audits require regeneration/review.

Historical write observations are not reproducible from stored snapshots. Admission
compares independent content checks and signatures while preserving historical
scope diagnostics; scope cannot be certified at read time or used for automatic
closure. Read-time UNKNOWNs do not become invented diagnostics. The journal tracks
production diagnostics only. Show reports freshness alongside the historical issue.

Operation cancellation records a rejected revision without resolving its issue.
Completed revision exports can be retried through issue resume. Committed decision
handoffs can be finalized after drift without replaying an already durable effect.

Pending issue resume additionally requires the exact operation and dependency
signature to match an explicit human record in the existing approval ledger.
Completed exports refuse a historical snapshot that no longer matches canonical
memory; retry cannot replace newer output with an old issue revision.
