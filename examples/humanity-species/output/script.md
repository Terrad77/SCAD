# Can Humanity Become a New Species?

**Logline:** When technology begins to shape evolution, the definition of 'human' may change forever.

Human evolution is no longer driven only by nature; technology is creating new forces of divergence.

## Hook

[FACT] Humans carry a small but measurable percentage of Neanderthal DNA from encounters tens of thousands of years ago. _(claims: CLM_001)_
[SPECULATION] But what if our species could split again — this time, driven by technology rather than ice and isolation? _(claims: CLM_003)_

## What is a biological species?

[FACT] In classical biology, a species is a group of organisms that can interbreed and produce fertile offspring.
[INTERPRETATION] The definition works well for well-isolated populations but blurs at the edges.

## Humans and Neanderthals

[FACT] Modern humans and Neanderthals interbred, leaving a small genetic legacy in non-African populations today. _(claims: CLM_001)_

## Technology as a new evolutionary force

[INTERPRETATION] CRISPR and other gene-editing tools could alter the human germline within a generation. _(claims: CLM_002)_
[SPECULATION] If heritable changes are introduced and propagate through a population, the result may be a new lineage. _(claims: CLM_003)_
